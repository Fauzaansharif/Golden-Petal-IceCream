our story

Quality ingredients. Remarkable taste. Immaculate service.

Golden Petal brings a warm, memorable ice cream experience to weddings, parties, and joyful gatherings.

Made to be part of your best moments

Golden Petal began with a love for carefully made ice cream — real cream, beautiful ingredients, and no shortcuts. Today, we bring that same care directly to the celebrations that matter most.

Our catering is designed around delight: a focused menu of signature flavours, friendly service, and an effortless dessert experience for hosts and guests alike.

— Akbarali & the Golden Petal family

what we stand for

Three things we never compromise on

🍓

Real ingredients, always

Fruit, cream, spice, dry-fruits and honey — sourced whole, never as flavoring or powder. If it's not something you'd cook with at home, it doesn't go in our churn.

🧊

A focused menu

We offer a curated choice of signature flavours, selected to make choosing easy and ensure there is something everyone enjoys.

🤝

Celebrations first

Our team is there to make your wedding, party, or gathering feel easy, personal, and delicious from the first scoop to the last.
